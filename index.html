Overshirts & layers

The Frame flannel is fine for daytime walking, pasta, and the truffle block — open over a tee with grey or green/brown jeans. It’s a notch too soft/cabin for Buca Lapi and Palazzo Gondi. Keep it as the day layer; don’t make it the only jacket.

Prefer a solid, structured overshirt — cleaner hand, no loud plaid, works open over a tee. One overshirt total is enough beside the blazer.

Wool / felted (best for Florence 60s)

  • Quince Italian Wool Overshirt Black or charcoal · ~$130 · shirt-jacket hybrid
  • FRAME Double Face Wool Shirt Jacket If he already likes Frame’s fit
  • Buck Mason Felted Field Shirt Merino · clean · less “shacket”

Cotton twill / canvas (cleaner than flannel, still casual)

  • Quince Brushed Cotton-Wool Utility Black/olive · inexpensive default
  • Buck Mason Craftsman Canvas Field Olive over brown/green jeans looks intentional

Skip for this trip

Plaid Frame overshirts · terry/texture novelty · suede (too precious for cobblestones + truffle mud)

  • Frame flannel → Sat daytime, Sun AM truffle/transfer, Mon morning walk
  • Blazer or wool overshirt → Fri dinner, Sun PM, Mon farewell
